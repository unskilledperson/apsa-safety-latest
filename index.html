Certified Professional Services

Scope of Work & Third-Party Inspections

Comprehensive mechanical examination, load testing, and proof certification adhering to UAE federal laws and EIAC accreditation requirements.

Mandatory Inspection

Lifting Equipment & Cranes

Complete proof load testing and statutory certification for mobile cranes, crawler cranes, overhead gantries, and tower cranes verifying structural stability, wire rope tension, and safe load indicators.

EIAC Accredited BS EN 12079 LOLER / PUWER
Mandatory Inspection

Chain Block & Lever Hoist

Ensures chain blocks and lever mechanisms are free from deformation, wear, and corrosion while verifying smooth lifting operation, brake holding capacity, and safety latches.

EN 13157 ASME B30.16 Periodic Proof Test
Mandatory Inspection

Window & BMU Cradles

Full examination of suspension wire ropes, platforms, hoists, safety arrest locks, and electrical controls used for high-rise facade access and exterior cleaning.

EN 1808 UAE Statutory Code Annual Recertification
Specialized Marine

Bollard Pull Tests & Winches

Carrying out bollard pull tests for offshore supply vessels, winches brake rendering proof tests, and load certification of towing and anchor handling winches.

IMCA Compliant DNV-GL Guidelines Offshore Certified
Specialized Marine

Spooling Machine Services

Spooling and unspooling of anchor handling & towing wire ropes onboard offshore vessels using our diesel-operated spooling units with hydraulic tension control.

Tension Calibrated Up to 80mm Rope Port & Anchorage
Material Integrity

NDT Testing (MPI / UT / PT)

Non-Destructive Testing via Magnetic Particle (MPI), Ultrasonic Testing (UT), and Liquid Penetrant (PT) to detect surface and subsurface weld or casting discontinuities.

ISO 9712 Level II ASME Sec V AWS D1.1

Professional HSE Development

Accredited Training Programs

Enhance your team's compliance and life-saving capabilities with certified, hands-on courses recognized by KHDA, EIAC, DCAS, and Highfield International.

2 Days • KHDA / DCAS

Fire Fighting & Fire Warden

Live fire simulator suppression drills, evacuation leadership, fire risk assessments, and proper operation of CO2, Foam, and Dry Chemical extinguishers.

1 Day • DCAS / Medic First Aid

First Aid, CPR & AED

Hands-on cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), automated external defibrillator (AED) operation, trauma triage, wound care, and fracture immobilization.

1-2 Days • EIAC / KHDA

Work at Heights & Fall Arrest

Comprehensive fall protection equipment inspection, 100% tie-off methodologies, horizontal lifeline rigging, and suspended harness trauma rescue procedures.

2-3 Days • EIAC / ENAS

Rigging & Lifting Operations

Sling angles, load weight estimation, center of gravity analysis, crane banksman hand signaling, and rejection criteria for loose lifting tackle.

2 Days • OSHA / EIAC

Confined Space Entry & Rescue

Multi-gas atmospheric testing, SCBA breathing equipment, retrieval tripod mechanical winches, permit-to-work systems, and emergency rescue simulations.

2-3 Days • Highfield / EIAC

Forklift & Heavy Plant Operator

Counterbalanced forklift maneuvering, stability triangle principles, high-bay stacking, pedestrian safety, pre-operational safety check audits, and practical testing.
